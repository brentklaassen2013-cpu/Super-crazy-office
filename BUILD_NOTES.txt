CRAZY OFFICE v0.61.0 — FINAL GAUNTLET BUILD

BASE
- Built from v0.60.0 Master Gauntlet.
- Keeps the full current Crazy Office game: combat, breakable bats, destructible/grabbable office props, NPC prop use, bosses, incidents, progression, cosmetics, crates, modes, multiplayer, adaptive audio and prior polish systems.

FINAL GAUNTLET PASSES
1. ENCOUNTER FAIRNESS
- Added a lightweight pressure director based on player HP, active enemies and boss state.
- When pressure becomes extreme, enemies briefly delay their next attack rather than the game secretly granting health or lowering earned difficulty.
- Low-health danger receives a restrained spatial cue instead of constant UI spam.

2. THREAT READABILITY
- Boss telegraphs and active arena warnings temporarily take priority over overlapping attacks.
- This reduces unfair damage during high-Mayhem moments without making bosses passive.

3. LONG-SESSION STABILITY
- Added defensive caps for temporary polish VFX and boss hazards.
- Extreme loose-prop linear/angular velocities are clamped to prevent runaway physics from accumulating during long standalone-headset sessions.
- Existing normal sleep/disposal systems remain primary; this is only a safety layer.

4. SYSTEM COHESION RETAINED
- NPC improvised melee and throws remain active.
- Host-authoritative multiplayer prop synchronization remains active.
- Run Promotions, Style, Mayhem, Office Incidents, boss phases/hazards, bat durability/break/reissue, object materials and dynamic music remain active.
